# Survival Korean
> Twenty phrases that carry you through a day — getting around, ordering, and the office.

## Getting around
이거 얼마예요? | How much is this? | i-geo eol-ma-ye-yo
화장실이 어디예요? | Where is the bathroom? | hwa-jang-sil-i eo-di-ye-yo
지하철역이 어디예요? | Where is the subway station? | ji-ha-cheol-yeok-i eo-di-ye-yo
여기서 내릴게요. | I'll get off here. | yeo-gi-seo nae-ril-ge-yo
천천히 말해 주세요. | Please speak slowly. | cheon-cheon-hi mal-hae ju-se-yo

## Coffee and food
커피 한 잔 주세요. | One coffee, please. | keo-pi han jan ju-se-yo
아이스 아메리카노 주세요. | An iced americano, please. | a-i-seu a-me-ri-ka-no ju-se-yo
포장해 주세요. | To go, please. | po-jang-hae ju-se-yo
맛있어요. | It's delicious. | ma-si-sseo-yo
계산할게요. | I'll pay now. | gye-san-hal-ge-yo

## At work
안녕하세요, 처음 뵙겠습니다. | Hello, nice to meet you. | an-nyeong-ha-se-yo, cheo-eum boep-gget-sseum-ni-da
잠시만요. | Just a moment. | jam-si-man-yo
확인해 볼게요. | I'll check on it. | hwa-gin-hae bol-ge-yo
수고하셨습니다. | Good work today. | su-go-ha-syeot-sseum-ni-da
내일 뵙겠습니다. | See you tomorrow. | nae-il boep-gget-sseum-ni-da

## Every day
괜찮아요. | It's okay. I'm fine. | gwaen-cha-na-yo
잘 모르겠어요. | I'm not sure. | jal mo-reu-ge-sseo-yo
다시 한번 말해 주세요. | Please say that again. | da-si han-beon mal-hae ju-se-yo
감사합니다. | Thank you. | gam-sa-ham-ni-da
죄송합니다. | I'm sorry. | joe-song-ham-ni-da
