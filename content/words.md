# Word of the Day
> Words worth having on the tip of your tongue.

## Set one
ubiquitous | Present everywhere at once. | Screens have become ubiquitous, even at the dinner table.
laconic | Using very few words. | His laconic reply was a single nod.
ameliorate | To make a bad situation better. | Nothing we did could ameliorate the delay.
tacit | Understood without being said. | There was a tacit agreement that nobody would mention it.
ephemeral | Lasting for a very short time. | The mist was ephemeral, gone by nine.
salient | The most noticeable or important. | Give me the salient points and skip the rest.

## Set two
recalcitrant | Stubbornly resisting authority. | The recalcitrant printer refused to acknowledge any of us.
sanguine | Cheerfully optimistic, especially in a hard situation. | She stayed sanguine even as the numbers came in.
perfunctory | Done with minimal effort, as a formality. | He gave the report a perfunctory glance.
inchoate | Just begun, and not yet fully formed. | The plan was inchoate, more mood than method.
equanimity | Calmness under strain. | He took the bad news with real equanimity.
germane | Genuinely relevant to the matter at hand. | That's interesting, but it isn't germane to the decision.
